Fonts in Arachne

142 families for the text tool, every one free and open source. Each family ships exactly as its designer published it, with the license file beside it, and a font only downloads when you pick it. Together they are 15142 KB.

Families come from the google/fonts repository at commit 5e35378e6bda, the same place the licenses link to. The group names below are Arachne's; the underlying tags are the repository's own.

Every family except the pixel faces also carries a sheet of letters drawn ahead of time by FreeType 2.14.3 at 8 to 19 blocks, for the hinted letter style. 44 families are fitted to the grid by their own hinting and 80 by FreeType's algorithm, because they carry none. A sheet downloads only when the style is used, and together they are 5247 KB.
